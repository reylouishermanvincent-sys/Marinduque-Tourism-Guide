WELCOME TO THE HEART OF THE PHILIPPINES

Find a favorite place along the way.

Marinduque is an island province in the MIMAROPA region. Its shorelines, natural landscapes, heritage sites, and lively traditions make it rewarding to explore at an unhurried pace. Browse the cards below for a quick introduction to six memorable stops.

BEACHES · NATURE · HERITAGE

Places worth exploring

Each card follows the same simple format: attraction name, location, and a short description.

Beach

Poctoy White Beach

Location: Torrijos, Marinduque

Description: Enjoy the white sand, clear waters, and relaxing coastal scenery.

Nature

Bathala Caves

Location: Santa Cruz, Marinduque

Description: Explore natural cave formations and appreciate the island's natural beauty.

Island scenery

Tres Reyes Islands

Location: Off the coast of Gasan, Marinduque

Description: Take in the scenic islets of Gaspar, Melchor, and Baltazar on a coastal boat trip.

Heritage

Boac Cathedral

Location: Boac, Marinduque

Description: Visit the historic hilltop church and stroll through the old town center.

Mountain landscape

Mount Malindig

Location: Southern Marinduque, near Buenavista and Torrijos

Description: Admire the island's prominent mountain and green countryside. Check trail access and local guidance before hiking.

Festival & tradition

Moriones Festival

Location: Marinduque towns, during Holy Week

Description: Experience a distinctive Lenten tradition known for colorful Morion masks and community processions.

Before your trip, check local travel advice, weather, transport schedules, and site access. Be respectful of local customs and natural places.
