A WARM ISLAND WELCOME

A small island with plenty to explore.

Marinduque sits in the MIMAROPA region of the Philippines and is known for its relaxed coastal scenery, historic town centers, and the colorful Moriones Festival. Whether you have a day or a longer stay, these highlights offer a mix of nature and culture.
